# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community.
We welcome participation regardless of background, identity, experience level,
or personal circumstances.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Focus discussion on ideas, evidence, and the project.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate
disruption, and publication of private information without permission are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to phil@lies.io. Reports will be handled
promptly and as confidentially as practical. Project maintainers may warn,
remove, or ban participants who violate this code.
